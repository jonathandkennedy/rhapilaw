Why the first offer is almost never the real number

An opening offer is a business decision, not a valuation. It is calculated on what an unrepresented person is likely to accept while they are frightened, in pain, and watching bills arrive. It typically accounts for the emergency room visit and very little else — not the physical therapy you have not started, not the MRI nobody has ordered yet, not the work you will miss, and not what the injury will still cost you in two years.

What goes into a full valuation

Medical bills already incurred and the cost of treatment you still need. Lost wages and reduced earning capacity. Property damage. Pain and suffering. Every applicable policy, including underinsured motorist coverage on your own policy that most people forget they have. Getting that complete picture is most of the work, and it is why cases settle for more once someone builds it properly.
