What comes up again and again

People, not a firm. Clients name individuals — Kyle, Abigail, Ismael, Felix, Corbin, Robert. That is unusual in personal injury, where most reviews describe a process. It happens here because the same people stay on your file from the first call to the settlement.

Someone answers. "If I ever had a question there was a number I could text… the responses were both prompt and informed." That is the single most repeated theme across 144 reviews, and it is the thing people complain about most at other firms.

No pressure. One reviewer above was referred elsewhere because the firm was not the right fit for his case, and he still left five stars. We would rather tell you the truth in a ten-minute call than sign a case we should not take.
